Key VSA Terminology Dictionary

Quick reference guide to critical Volume Spread Analysis and Wyckoff concepts.

Upthrust (UT / UTAD) BEARISH TRAP
A sharp price push above resistance on high volume that immediately closes back below the barrier, confirming institutional supply dumping.
Spring (Phase C) BULLISH TEST
A temporary breakdown below support designed to trigger stops and capture liquidity before an impulsive markup campaign begins.
Stopping Volume ACCUMULATION
Ultra-high volume appearing during a downtrend where the bar fails to produce downside follow-through, indicating Smart Money absorption.
No Demand / No Supply Bar VALIDATION
Narrow spread candles on noticeably low volume, confirming an absence of counter-trend interest and clearing the path for trend resumption.
