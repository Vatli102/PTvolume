Privacy Policy

Commitment to transparent data governance, user confidentiality, and encryption standards.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

PTvolume.com operates as an open-access educational repository. We firmly believe in reader privacy:

  • We never ask for financial account details, broker passwords, crypto private keys, or sensitive personal credentials.
  • Access to our educational articles, technical analyses, and classic book reviews is unrestricted and does not require credit card information.

2. Cookies & Anonymous Analytics

To maintain server performance, analyze aggregated traffic trends, and deliver interactive charting widgets (such as TradingView), our website may utilize standard session cookies and anonymous CDN telemetry (Cloudflare / GitHub Pages).

3. Secure Encryption Standards

All communications between your client device and our servers are encrypted end-to-end using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). We never sell, rent, or distribute user data to third-party marketing entities.

Our articles and resources may link to third-party tools, broker platforms, or research references. PTvolume is not responsible for the privacy practices or contents of these external domains.
