Mission & Editorial Philosophy

PTvolume.com was established with a singular objective: to deliver institutional-grade market education and empirical technical research to independent retail traders across the globe.

Most modern retail trading education is saturated with derivative lagging indicators, ambiguous chart overlays, and gambling mindsets. At PTvolume, we strip away all market noise to focus exclusively on the two foundational truths of any liquid market: Price Action and Volume.

"Markets do not move on mathematical formulas or emotional sentiment; they move strictly according to the interplay of Supply and Demand orchestrated by institutional composite operators."
